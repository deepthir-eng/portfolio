Robotic Hand

Flex Sensor Based Assistive Robotic Hand

Designed and developed a robotic hand that senses finger bending through five flex sensors and reproduces the movement through five servo motors controlled by an Arduino-based system.

Arduino UNO5 Flex Sensors5 Servo Motors C/C++Analog SignalsPWM

What I worked on

  • Developed embedded control logic for real-time sensor-to-servo response.
  • Interfaced and calibrated five flex sensors for finger movement detection.
  • Implemented sensor smoothing to reduce jitter and improve response.
  • Handled hardware-software integration, debugging and system validation.
